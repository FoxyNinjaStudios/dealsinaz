  • Stay for up to two guests in a Fairmont King, Sunset Signature King, or Casita Suite King.

About Christmas at the Princess #

Back for another spectacular holiday season, Christmas at the Princess invites one and all to its annual festival celebrating the magic of winter. This annual event provides multiple attractions and great opportunities to bond with family in an environment of holiday cheer. There’s plenty of icy fun to be had, like skating in the Desert Ice Skating Rink, or creating holiday stuffed animals at the Build-A-Bear Workshop. Lights are aglow throughout the land, especially aboard the Lagoon Lights and Princess Express Trains that travel from one end of the resort to the other. And, with the addition of an HD video show at the Enchanted Plaza Show, lights are a main attraction for holiday fun. Also, for a sweet treat to last the night, families can enjoy treats made at S’mores Land and roast marshmallows around the campfire in a Christmas Tree Forest. From a Christmas Carousel to Frosty’s Ferris Wheel, and even a special visit with Santa, Christmas at the Princess makes each Christmas season one to remember.”
